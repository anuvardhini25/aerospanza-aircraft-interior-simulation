## Problem Statement

Embraer aims to design sustainable aircraft interiors that reduce environmental impact while maintaining passenger comfort and aesthetic appeal.

### Key Challenges
- Sourcing **eco-friendly materials** suitable for aircraft interiors.
- Ensuring **durability and safety standards**.
- Maintaining **cost-effectiveness**.
- Reducing the **environmental impact** of aircraft interior components.

The objective is to create aircraft interiors that are **sustainable, efficient, and comfortable for passengers**.

---

## Proposed Solution

- Use **eco-friendly materials** such as recycled composites, biodegradable fabrics, and bio-based foams.
- Implement a **modular design approach** for easy replacement and repair of interior components.
- Incorporate **lightweight materials and structures** to improve fuel efficiency.
- Utilize **3D printing technology** for efficient production and customization of aircraft interior parts.
- Integrate **energy-efficient systems** such as LED lighting and smart cabin technologies.
- Design interiors for **easy disassembly and recycling** to support a circular economy.

## Hackathon Rounds

### Round 1 – Quiz Round
Date: **20 August 2024**

The first round consisted of an online quiz conducted through the **Unstop platform**.  
Participants answered **40 multiple-choice questions related to aviation** within a time limit of **20 minutes**.  
This round tested participants' knowledge of aviation concepts and industry fundamentals.

---

### Round 2 – Case Study Analysis
Date: **22 August – 24 August 2024**

Teams were assigned **five real-world aviation case studies**.  
Participants conducted research using industry reports, databases, and academic resources.

Teams submitted a document describing:
- Case study analysis
- Research findings
- Proposed solutions

---

### Round 3 – Design PPT Submission
Date: **28 August – 30 August 2024**

Participants selected a problem statement related to aviation challenges and developed an innovative solution.

Teams submitted a **PowerPoint presentation** explaining:
- Problem analysis
- Sustainable design approach
- Proposed technical solution
- Expected benefits and impact

Submissions were evaluated based on **innovation, feasibility, and technical clarity**.

---

### Round 4 – Prototype Design and Simulation
Date: **2 September – 6 September 2024**

In this round, the proposed solution was developed into a **prototype model**.

The prototype was simulated using **Scilab** to evaluate different aspects of the sustainable aircraft interior design.

The simulations included:
- Energy consumption over time
- Modular seating system lifetime
- Pollutant level variation
- Component lifetime with modular repair design

These simulations generated **3D graphical models** that demonstrated the feasibility and sustainability of the proposed solution.
